## v0.95.2, the same evening — a question on two lines never reached WhatsApp

Tchiya (a member) made a circle, London, added Rakefet by WhatsApp and asked
*"Would love to get recommendation for hotel in London,⏎Thank you"* — she
pressed Enter mid-question. Rakefet got nothing, and the screen showed Meta's
raw JSON, cut off mid-word.

- **Cause, measured:** Meta refuses a template parameter containing a new-line,
  a tab or more than four consecutive spaces (error **132018**), and refuses
  the whole message. `send-query` passed the question through with `.trim()`,
  which only touches the ends. 1 of 77 questions ever asked had a line break;
  hers is the only delivery it failed.
- **Fix:** `waParam` in `_shared/channels.ts` flattens every WhatsApp parameter
  in the one sender everything goes through — breaks and tabs to a space, runs
  of spaces to one, capped at 700 characters (the whole template body is
  limited to 1024). The stored question keeps its line break. Deployed:
  `send-query` v79, `resend-member` v60, `send-collection` v55 — each
  downloaded afterwards and diffed equal to git, `verify_jwt` still on.
  (`send-collection` only uses the shared file for email: a list goes out as a
  wa.me link the sender sends themselves, where line breaks are fine. I said
  otherwise to dan before reading the source, and corrected it.)
- **A failed delivery now reads as a sentence** — `deliveryErrorText`, in the
  delivery strip, the Resend toasts and the list-sending result. Meta's codes
  mapped: 132018, 131026, 131030, 100/131008/131009, 130429/131056/131048, 190,
  1320xx; email and "no way to reach them" too. The raw text stays folded under
  **Details**.
- `delivery-errors-sim.js`: her exact question and her exact error, from the
  production row; 36 checks, control fails 7. Merged as `830e970`.
- **Rakefet still has not got the question.** Tchiya can press Resend beside
  her name on that screen (email box empty = WhatsApp again), or ask again.

## What changed

**The design system**, as a layer at the end of the stylesheet (`REDESIGN
LAYER`), so the rules above it — several of which carry their own history —
are overridden, not deleted. One deep green for anything you can press; the
phone's own serif (`ui-serif`, New York on iPhone) for the wordmark and
headlines; filled fields; pill buttons; dialogs as bottom sheets on a phone;
new outline tab icons; section labels in small capitals.

**Screens rebuilt:** Home and the first-run steps, the ☰ menu (new), the ➕
sheet, Circles, a circle, Library rows (`recCardHtml` — every list of things
uses it), Inbox, Add friends with Paste a number, the Ask screen, the Save
sheet, the answer sheet, sign-in and the invitation.
**Restyled only** (the design system, no new layout): a question's page, an
item's page, Profile, Settings, Answered, Taste Match, Add circle.

**Home** lost: the three counters; "Recent from your network", which outside
the retired demo listed *your own library* under that label; the Taste Match
banner; the Profile card. Home now reads: ask box, your circles as chips, the
one question waiting on *your* answer, your open questions as cards, what
people shared.

**Paste a number** (`handlePasteNumber`, `pastedPhone`) reads the clipboard,
strips the invisible direction marks Hebrew phones wrap numbers in, parses with
the existing `toE164`, and **fills the same form "+ Add someone new" opens** —
so the server's identity rules decide who the person is, exactly as before. If
the browser refuses the clipboard, the number box opens with "press and hold,
then Paste".

## Bugs found on the way — three of them live today

1. **The Ask screen threw on every render (live).** `initQueryView` called
   `setDegree`, which called `updateRouting`, which read `qCircle` — declared
   with `const` five lines further down. "Cannot access 'qCircle' before
   initialization". Everything after it never ran: **the Degree 1 / Degree 2
   buttons have had no listeners**, and the routing preview never drew. No one
   reported it; the harness found it the first time it drew the screen.
2. **The app loaded a Google font (live).** Heebo, since the v0.20.0 reskin,
   despite the client rule against external imports. Removed; the body is the
   system font the mockups were approved in.
3. **USER-FLOWS stuck points #5, #6, #7 (live)** — the typed question lost on
   switching verb, an item sent that was no longer on screen, doors that did
   not set the verb. Fixed; see the note at the top of that list.

And two of mine, caught before they shipped: `circleCardHtml` ended up declared
twice and **the old one silently won** (a later declaration always does); and
an icon with no size drew at 300×150 and crushed its row.

## How it was tested

**`redesign-browser-sim.js` — 76 assertions, control fails.** It loads the
real page in headless Chrome at phone width with realistic data, draws 27
screens, and then *uses* the ones that changed: reads the first-run steps in
three states, pastes a number with invisible marks in it, refuses the
clipboard, flips Degree 2, types a question and switches verb, picks a circle
and turns on send. Its control, `index.pre-v0.95.0.html`, fails with the
exact live errors.

Two things about it worth knowing before trusting any screenshot here:

- **Headless Chrome on Windows will not open a window narrower than ~512px.**
  `--window-size=390` silently lays the page out at 512 and crops it — the
  first "phone" screenshots of this work were wrong for exactly that reason.
  The sim draws the page inside a 390px iframe instead.
- **Its first version checked the page's text for the old Taste Match banner
  and failed — on its own words.** The test script is injected into the page,
  so `document.body.textContent` contained the phrase it was looking for. It
  reads only `#view-body` now. Written the other way round, it would have
  passed for the wrong reason.

**The whole suite, before and after, diffed:** every sim that passed on
v0.94.1 still passes, every control still fails. Two needed attention and the
reasons are recorded in each: `save-and-send-sim` greps for the literal
`data-action="fab-save"`, which the new sheet built from a variable (now
written out whole); `personal-category-sim` pinned dining's old colour, and the
palette changed deliberately.

## What is NOT done — say so before anyone assumes it

- **Not seen on a real phone.** Everything above is headless Chrome. Safari's
  and WhatsApp's in-app browser's rendering of the bottom sheets, the serif and
  the clipboard read are unverified. The clipboard read in WhatsApp's browser
  especially: if it is refused, the fallback path is what people get.
- **"You're in" — circles you belong to but do not own — is not built.** The
  mockup had it. Nothing in the client loads those circles; it needs a server
  query first.
- **The secondary screens** (a question's page, an item's page, Profile,
  Settings, Answered, Taste Match) have the new look but their old layout.
- **The desktop sidebar is the old dark design**, with renamed labels.
- `docs/BETA-SMOKE-TEST.md` has **not** been walked on this build.
